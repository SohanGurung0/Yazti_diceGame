How to Play Yahtzee

Yahtzee is a classic dice game played with five dice. Each turn follows the same three-step structure:

  1. Roll all five dice. You start each turn with up to three rolls.
  2. Keep the dice you want. After the first or second roll, click any die to hold it — held dice are not re-rolled.
  3. Score your roll. Open the scorecard and choose a category that fits your dice. Each category can only be used once per game.

The game ends when all 13 scoring categories are filled. The player with the highest total wins.

Yahtzee Scoring

There are 13 scoring categories in two sections. Score 63 or more in the Upper Section to earn a 35-point bonus.

Yahtzee All five dice the same 50 pts

Tips for Getting a Higher Yahtzee Score

  • Aim for the upper bonus early. You need roughly three of each number to reach 63. Prioritise Sixes and Fives first, they score the most per die.
  • Don't waste Chance too early. It accepts any roll, making it a useful safety net when nothing else fits. Save it for late in the game.
  • Hold pairs aggressively on roll one. A pair gives you a foundation for Three of a Kind, Four of a Kind, Full House, or Yahtzee on subsequent rolls.
  • Know when to sacrifice a category. If you have no good options, score a zero in Ones or Twos rather than burning a high-value lower-section slot.
  • Watch your opponent's scorecard. In 2-player mode, knowing which categories your opponent still needs can influence which dice you chase.
